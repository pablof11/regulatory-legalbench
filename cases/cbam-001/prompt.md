# CBAM-001 — Reliance on Incorrect Actual Embedded Emissions Data

## Scenario

An EU importer, acting as the authorised CBAM declarant, imports steel during 2026 from an installation operated by a company established in a third country.

The importer chooses to determine the embedded emissions of the imported goods using actual emissions because this results in a lower number of CBAM certificates than the applicable default values.

The operator prepares the emissions report using primary data from its production processes. The reported embedded emissions are verified by a verifier accredited pursuant to Article 18 of Regulation (EU) 2023/956. The verifier issues the corresponding verification report.

The importer receives the operator’s emissions information and the verification report. The documents appear complete and valid on their face. The importer does not have independent access to the installation, its production systems, or the underlying industrial processes and cannot directly reproduce all the primary data used in the calculation.

By 30 September 2027, the importer submits its CBAM declaration and surrenders the number of CBAM certificates calculated using the verified actual emissions.

The competent authority subsequently determines that information originating from the operator and reflected in the verification process was incorrect. The error materially understated the actual embedded emissions and caused the importer to surrender fewer CBAM certificates than were legally required.

There is no evidence that the importer colluded with the operator or verifier or deliberately submitted false information. No further facts are provided concerning the importer’s conduct, internal controls, or contractual arrangements with the operator or verifier.

## Task

You are acting as senior EU customs and regulatory counsel advising the importer.

Prepare a reasoned legal memorandum determining the regulatory consequences of the understatement, analysing the respective legal positions of the authorised CBAM declarant, operator and verifier, and advising the importer on its exposure and available arguments.

Identify any additional facts necessary to reach a fully supported conclusion.
